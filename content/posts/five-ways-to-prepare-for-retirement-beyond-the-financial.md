You’re almost there, almost ready to quit the 9 to 5, and start the next great adventure – but while financial considerations are often the focus of retirement planning, it’s also important to think about other aspects of retirement. Here are five ways to prepare for retirement beyond the financial considerations:

## Think about your lifestyle

Retirement is an opportunity to pursue new hobbies, travel, and spend time with loved ones. Start thinking about the lifestyle you want to have in retirement and plan accordingly. Consider the activities you enjoy, the places you want to visit, and the people you want to spend time with. This will help you create a retirement plan that is tailored to your interests and needs.

## Prioritize your health

As you approach retirement, it’s important to prioritize your health. This means taking care of your physical, mental, and emotional well-being. Make sure to schedule regular check-ups with your healthcare provider, eat a healthy diet, and stay active. Consider joining a gym or taking fitness classes to stay in shape. Additionally, prioritize your mental health by staying socially engaged, learning new things, and practicing mindfulness and relaxation techniques.

## Build your social network

Retirement can be a major life transition, and having a strong social network can make all the difference. Make sure to stay connected with family and friends, and consider joining social groups or clubs to meet new people. Retirement villages (including ours!) often offer social events and activities, which can be a great way to make new friends and form new connections.

## Create a sense of purpose

Retirement can be an opportunity to pursue new interests and passions. Consider volunteering or getting involved in your community to create a sense of purpose and make a difference in the world. You can also pursue hobbies or interests that you may not have had time for before, such as writing, painting, or playing music.

## Plan for your living situation

As you approach retirement, it’s important to think about your living situation. Do you want to stay in your current home, downsize, or move to a retirement community? Consider the pros and cons of each option and make a plan that works for you. If you’re considering a retirement community, make sure to research different options and visit each community to get a sense of the amenities, lifestyle, and culture.

Preparing for retirement is about more than just finances. By thinking about your lifestyle, health, social network, sense of purpose, and living situation, you can create a retirement plan that is tailored to your needs and interests. With a little planning and preparation, you can enjoy a fulfilling and rewarding retirement.
