Family first

Keeping families and loved ones together

The close proximity to Highgrove lets couples and community friends stay together and connected, even when one resident needs a more intensive level of care.

Consistent with our family-first ethos, Patrick Ferry House was designed to be closely connected with the independent living apartments — literally just a short internal walk away.

Our care

Comfortable, familiar, and close to home

01

Aged care

We offer day, respite, rehabilitation and recovery, full rest home and palliative care. Our Registered Nurses and Healthcare Assistants support residents while encouraging dignity and independence.

02

Feel at home

Modern single rooms, each with a private ensuite and access to a Mediterranean courtyard or individual balcony — plus emergency response systems for comfort and security.

03

Comfortable surroundings

Four comfortable lounges for residents and their families, with a dining room and kitchenette on each floor, so a cup of tea is always close at hand.
